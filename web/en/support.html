Support

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Write to us

Questions, ideas or problems: we usually reply within 1 business day.

Send an email

Write to us about any question, idea or problem with Expeat. We usually reply within 1 business day.

Contact

Email: destek@puanla.app

In the app: Settings > Contact us.

Reporting content and users

Choose “Report” from the … menu on any post, comment or profile. We review reports within 24 hours.

From the same menu you can block someone; people you block can’t see you or your content. You can unblock people in Settings > Blocked people.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and all your data via Settings > Delete account.

Missing or incorrect places

Use “Add a new place” in search to add a missing place, or email us about incorrect details.
