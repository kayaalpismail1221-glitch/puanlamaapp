Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your Expeat account and all of your data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Expeat and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the settings (gear) icon in the top right.
  3. Tap “Delete account” and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately; this cannot be undone.

If you can’t access the app

Email destek@puanla.app from your registered email address with the subject “Account deletion” and include your username. Once we verify the account is yours, it is deleted within 30 days and we let you know.

Send a deletion request

What gets deleted

Your profile, ratings and rankings, posts, photos, comments, lists, follow connections, invites and all other data tied to your account are permanently deleted.

What may be kept

Records we are legally required to keep are retained only for the period the law requires. See “Retention and deletion” in the Privacy Policy for details.
